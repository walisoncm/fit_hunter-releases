## Release Notes

# ✨ Fit Hunter v1.5.0 — Fasting Overhaul

## 🆕 What's New

- **Log your actual meal/break time**: starting or finishing a fast no longer has to happen at the exact moment you open the app. Pick "Now," a quick shortcut like "-1h," or a full date & time — so a fast you started or broke a few hours (or days) ago still tracks accurately.
- **Fasting stages reference**: tap the biological-effect box during a fast to see all 7 stages and the hour range each one kicks in, with your current one highlighted.
- **Fasting history**: a new history screen (tap the clock icon next to the Fasting Tracker) shows your past fasts — start/end time, duration, and which biological stage you reached.
- **Cloud backup now covers more than chat**: your health and fasting history are now included in your encrypted Google Drive backup alongside your messages, so restoring after a reinstall brings back more of your data.

## 🛠️ Improvements & Fixes

- "Delete for me" and "Clear chat" in a direct message conversation now also remove messages that were already cached locally — previously they only affected messages arriving after the action, so most of a conversation's history was untouched.
- The fasting elapsed timer now freezes while you're picking when you broke your fast, instead of visibly ticking up behind the dialog as if the fast were still running.
