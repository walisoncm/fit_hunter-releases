## Release Notes

# ✨ Fit Hunter v1.2.0 — Exercise Tracking, Right in the App

## 🆕 What's New

- **In-App Exercise Tracking**: start and track workouts directly in Fit Hunter — with a live GPS map for outdoor activities, pause/resume, and automatic Health Connect logging (calories, distance, steps, and route) when you finish.
- Exercise sessions survive an unexpected app close: reopening the app lets you resume, finish, or discard right where you left off.

## 🛠️ Improvements & Fixes

- Fixed the calorie value shown when sharing exercises, which could vary between refreshes when Health Connect held duplicate records.
- Fixed Google Sign-In sometimes asking for your account twice or failing with an unexplained error.
- Basketball and soccer now have distinct icons.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

- The hidden tester login now requires a deliberate sustained hold, reducing the risk of accidental activation.
