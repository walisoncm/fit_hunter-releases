## Release Notes

# ✨ Fit Hunter v1.4.0 — Best Friends & Workout Duels

## 🆕 What's New

- **Best Friends**: mark a friend as your best friend and get a push notification the moment they start an in-app workout — perfect for jumping in and training together.
- **Workout Duels**: challenge friends or your whole group/guild/party to a synchronized workout. Everyone accepts, gets ready, and starts together after a countdown — then watch everyone's live stats update in real time as the race unfolds.
- **Workout Sharing**: share any finished workout (tracked in-app or synced from a smartwatch/other app) straight into a chat, complete with its route map, duration, calories, and distance.
- **Ghost Challenges**: race against a shared completed workout — your live session is compared step-by-step against a simulated replay of the original, with the ghost's path projected onto your own route and elevation taken into account when available.

## 🛠️ Improvements & Fixes

- Sharing a workout with a recorded route now also shows a mini-map, matching the auto check-in card.

## 📚 Behind the Scenes

- This release went through extra scrutiny before shipping — a dedicated bug/duplication review pass caught and fixed a case where blocking someone mid-duel could permanently strand you from creating or joining any future duel.
