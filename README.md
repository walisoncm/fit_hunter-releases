# ✨ Fit Hunter v1.1.0 — Stability & Security Update

## 🆕 What's New

- **Android Home Screen Widget**: track your Daily Quest progress right from your home screen.
- **Encrypted Cloud Backup**: back up your hunter progress to Google Drive and restore it on any device.
- **Penalty Reversal**: quest penalties are now automatically reversed when late-arriving activity data proves you actually hit your goal.
- **Share to Stories**: share links and text directly to your Story.
- **Party Challenge History**: see your party's completed challenges and how you rank against other members.

## 🛠️ Improvements & Fixes

- Numerous chat, guild, and party sync and reliability fixes.
- Smoother performance across dungeons, the System HUD, and social screens.
- Full translation coverage — no more missing or mixed-language text.
- Various crash and edge-case fixes.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

- Continued hardening of account, guild, and party permissions.
- Chat media and Stories are now served through private, signed links.
- General security review and hardening across the backend.
