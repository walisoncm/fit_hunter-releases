# ✨ Fit Hunter v1.3.0 — Exercise History & Live Map

## 🆕 What's New

- **Exercise History**: browse the last 7 days of workouts right from the Status screen, paging day by day — tap any workout to see its full details.

## 🛠️ Improvements & Fixes

- The live exercise session map now shows real streets and terrain (instead of just a line), auto-zooming with your speed, with manual pan/zoom available too.
- The route map on a workout's detail screen now also shows a real map, fit to the whole route.
- Fixed the in-progress exercise notification showing a blank icon in the status bar.
- "Today's Exercises" now sits right below the Daily Quest panel.

## 📚 Behind the Scenes

- This release went through extra scrutiny before shipping — 10 rounds of automated review caught cache, performance, and edge-case issues that didn't affect the visible experience but make the app sturdier under the hood.
